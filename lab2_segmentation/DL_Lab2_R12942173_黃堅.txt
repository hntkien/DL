## Dataset Pre-processing

`OxfordPetDataset` in `src/oxford_pet.py` loads split IDs, images, and masks, and checks for missing files. Masks: trimap-to-binary conversion, nearest-neighbor resize, tensor conversion, and center-crop to UNet output size (input 572, output 388). Images: resize, tensor conversion, and ImageNet mean/std normalization. Training adds photometric/appearance augmentations (distortion, posterize, sharpness, Gaussian blur) plus horizontal/vertical flips applied with masks.

## Model Architecture

1. U-Net (`src/models/unet.py`): original unpadded convs, center-crop skip concat, Kaiming init.
2. ResNet34-UNet + CBAM (`src/models/resnet34_unet.py`): ResNet34 encoder (stem + blocks [3,4,6,3]), 1x1 bottleneck concat with deepest features, decoder with bilinear upsample, 32-channel projection, skip concat, conv refinement, CBAM attention, and a final head to logits.

## Training Strategy 

Shared pipeline in `src/train.py` for both models. Loss combines BCE and Dice to balance pixel accuracy and region overlap; Dice is weighted higher (0.8). Optimization uses AdamW with lr 1e-3 and weight decay 1e-4. Mixed precision is enabled to fit 8GB GPU memory and speed up training. A cosine annealing scheduler smooths LR decay across epochs. Early stopping monitors validation Dice with patience 10. Training can resume from checkpoints if interrupted.
